# Capstone Project

## M09 - Capstone Project - Lesson Plan

This workshop guides learners through the final preparation, development, and execution of their capstone projects. Learners will review presentation standards, explore guided repository templates (Classification, Regression, and Time Series), and receive hands-on 1:1 guidance to finalize their end-to-end data science projects.

Learning Objectives and Learner Outcomes

| Learning Objective | Learner Outcome |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1. Understand capstone expectations, presentation criteria, and submission workflows** | **LO1:** Articulate key components of a successful 3-minute capstone presentation and locate Canvas submission guidelines.<br>**LO2:** Select an appropriate problem domain (Classification, Regression, or Time Series) matching individual project goals. |

Lesson Plan

| Timing (Mins) | Activity | Format | Content & Instructions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 15 (15) | Welcome & Session Introduction | Instructor Lead | **Content:** Welcome learners and outline the structure of the day. Clarify that the goal is to provide clarity on capstone requirements and offer hands-on project support.<br>**Instructions:** Confirm expected presentation length (3 mins), required content, submission deadline, and graduation location with coordinators (Karl/James/Boston) prior to starting. Set up the two-facilitator model (one main room anchor, one roving tutor). |
| 15 (30) | Capstone Presentation Requirements | Instructor Lead | **Content:** Walk through the Capstone Presentation Google Slides.<br>**Instructions:** Outline what constitutes a successful presentation, focusing on business context, data preparation, modeling choices, evaluation, and key insights. |
| 15 (45) | Presentation Exemplars & Submission Workflow | Instructor Lead & Demo | **Content:** Show examples of previous high-quality capstone presentations and explain Canvas submission steps.<br>**Instructions:** Demonstrate how to submit files on Canvas. Reiterate that final work must be submitted no later than 7 days prior to graduation. |
| 105 (2 hr 30) | Template Walkthrough & Initial Working Session | Instructor Lead & Breakout Support | **Content:** Introduce capstone templates and directory structure (`week 9/` containing `activity.py`, `notebook.ipynb`, and `exercises/` scripts).<br>**Instructions:** Ensure all learners copy teacher resources into their `My Work` folder. Transition learners into domain-specific breakout rooms (Classification, Regression, Time Series) once ready. Roving tutor provides initial setup assistance. |
| 60 (3 hr 30) | Lunch Break | N/A | **Content:** Rest and reset[cite: 1, 2].<br>**Instructions:** Encourage learners to step away from screens before afternoon independent coding. |
| 180 (6 hr 30) | Independent Capstone Session & 1:1 Support | Roving Support / Main Room Walkthrough | **Content:** Dedicated project development session. Main room hosts end-to-end template walkthroughs while breakout rooms provide focused working spaces.<br>**Instructions:** One tutor remains in the main room delivering walkthroughs; the second tutor moves between domain breakout rooms for 1:1 sessions. Log all individual support sessions (10–15+ mins) in the 1:1 tracking sheet with learner name and chosen problem type. |
| 30 (7 hr) | Wrap-up, Q&A & Deadline Recap | Whole Group | **Content:** Summarize key achievements of the day, answer remaining technical/administrative questions, and review deadlines.<br>**Instructions:** Remind learners of the final submission cutoff (7 days before graduation). Direct learners to remaining support channels for final queries. |
